What to expect at your first visit

  1. Book your appointment. Call (604) 261-4607. If you call between 9am and 1pm, we can often see you the same day.
  2. Talk to Dr. Li. A free phone consult lets you share your concerns before you come in.
  3. Exam and personal plan. We get to know you, assess your oral health and agree on a plan together.
  4. Treatment and follow-up. Dr. Li provides your care and remains your dentist for every future visit.
