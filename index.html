Our services

Implant dentistry, planned around you

From a single missing tooth to a full arch, each treatment is planned around your oral health, how you eat and speak, and how you want your smile to look.

Single-Tooth Implants

Replace one missing tooth with an implant and crown that look and function like a natural tooth.

Learn more about Single-Tooth Implants

Multiple Implants

Restore several missing teeth with implants planned around your bite and the teeth you still have.

Learn more about Multiple Implants

All-on-X Full-Arch Treatment

Why Maple Dental

Dental care the way we would want to be treated

The same dentist, every time

You see Dr. Stephen Li at every visit. Continuity of care means he knows your history, your goals and how you like to be treated.

A direct line to your dentist

Ask for Dr. Li whenever you need to talk. A phone conversation is often all it takes to answer a question or ease a worry, and you can reach us after hours, on weekends and on holidays. [CONFIRM after-hours process]

We speak your language

Our team speaks English, French, Chinese and Arabic, so you can discuss your care in the language you are most comfortable with.

Treatment only when it helps you

Dentistry offers many options. We explain them clearly and recommend only what benefits your oral and overall health.
